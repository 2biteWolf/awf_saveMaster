01. Open the extension and pick a language

Saves pictures and videos from any page with one key. Settings are in the popup.

1

Click the extension icon.

2

Language of the popup and on-page menus. Green = active.

3

Click a title to open it; only one block stays open. Click again to close. The last open block is remembered.

02. Save a picture with one key

1

Point at a picture. The green frame shows what will be saved.

2

Tap Ctrl+K.

03. Hold menu: Q W A S D

1

Hold Ctrl+K ~1 s. The menu opens centered on the cursor.

05. Layer Scalpel

Steps through the page layer by layer at one point and saves the layer you pick. The page is not changed.

1

Hold Ctrl+K, press D.

2

A purple crosshair appears in the middle and follows the mouse; the panel is at the bottom.

3

Point at the picture. The purple frame = current layer (here: a transparent overlay).

4

Deeper: wheel, →/↓, ]. Back: ←/↑, [. Or click a segment. Picture or video → green ✓.

5

Segments: purple = current, grey = passed, green line = picture. Then layer number, element name and size.

6

Click the page or Space to lock the aim; the mouse can now go to the panel. Click again to unlock.

7

Save: S, Enter, save or a tap of Ctrl+K. The scalpel closes, then saves. No picture on the layer → note only.

Esc or close close it and remove everything it drew. While open, page clicks and keys are blocked.

06. Look under overlays (automatic)

For sites that put an invisible button or frame over the picture.

1

In Behavior. Default: left button.

2

Each empty save counts as a miss for that spot.

3

3rd miss in the same spot → it looks under the overlays, saves the picture and remembers the site.

4

From then on: look-under always on for this site (hover frame too), saved on the first tap.

Do not look under overlays turns this off. Remembered sites travel with Download a settings file / Load a settings file. One-off cases: Layer Scalpel.

07. Zoom

1

Point at a picture and hold the mouse wheel (middle button) for Hold the wheel to zoom (Timings).

2

Slider on the right: max … 0. Bottom (0) closes zoom.

3

Wheel: closer / farther (100–300%). Drag to move.

4

Close: Esc or slider to 0.

08. Switching tabs

1

Quick menu open (Ctrl+K held) + wheel: one tab left/right.

2

Tab menu open (second hold) + wheel: TAB SPIN mode — the wheel keeps switching tabs, badge SPIN. Exit: click or the save key.

3

On picture-only tabs (viewer, image files) the wheel switches tabs. On/off: Wheel switches tabs in viewer (Behavior).

09. Viewer

1

Viewer tab.

2

3

4

5

History panel; click an entry to open it.

6

Esc — close.
